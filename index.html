DScraper: Paid Media lead agent

Finds local businesses with missing or weak paid media, and explains why each one is a good lead.

  • Scans a homepage for Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn and Microsoft ad tags.
  • Flags "weak paid media": ad tags with no conversion events, no Consent Mode, a single platform, no analytics or a weak landing page.
  • Scores higher when the site can turn clicks into calls (HTTPS, mobile-ready, phone, WhatsApp, form or booking).
  • Ranks every lead and exports the evidence to CSV and JSON.

DScraper: Website lead finder

Turns map listings into a ranked list of businesses that need a better online presence.

  • Covers 24 local-service categories, from dentists to roofers, with English, Spanish and French names.
  • Searches a city, a set of provinces, or all 52 provinces of Spain.
  • Detects outdated or dead websites, missing social profiles and missing online booking.
  • Uses public data only, respects rate limits, keeps an opt-out list and never contacts a lead. Long runs can be paused and resumed.

AutoEmployer: job search automation

An n8n workflow that finds remote jobs every morning, filters them with AI and tailors a CV for each match.

  • Pulls offers from 6 sources (Himalayas, Jobicy, Remotive, RemoteOK, We Work Remotely and JSearch) into one common format.
  • Cleans and de-duplicates them: in one tested run, 185 raw offers became 21 recent, unique ones.
  • Filters with rules plus an LLM evaluation (Gemini) using structured JSON output.
  • Generates a tailored CV through the Google Docs and Drive APIs, exported to PDF and sent for approval by email.

AI phone receptionist

A voice agent that answers a business's calls in Spanish, books appointments and captures leads.

  • Speech-to-text, an LLM and a natural voice, connected to n8n webhooks.
  • Books into Google Calendar, logs every call to Google Sheets, sends alerts and can transfer to a human.
  • Every call ends with one clear outcome: booked, lead captured, transferred or out of scope.
  • One config file per client, so a new business can be set up in under an hour.
